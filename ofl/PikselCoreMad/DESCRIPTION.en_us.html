Piksel Core Mad

Experimental pixel font for glitchy, modular designs.

Piksel Core Mad is an experimental pixel typeface designed on a strict square grid — with no curves, no diagonals, and no apologies. Every character is modular and sharp, but with deliberate glitches and visual tension. It includes uppercase and lowercase letters — a rare feature in pixel fonts — and comes in six weights (200–700).

Piksel Core Mad is ideal for experimental layouts, cyberpunk and glitch-inspired visuals, zines, posters, overlays, and projects that embrace raw digital aesthetics.
